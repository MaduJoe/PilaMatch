## Article 12 (Contract Status Management)

Contracts through the Service go through the following statuses sequentially:

```
CONFIRMED
  -> IN_PROGRESS or CANCELLED
     -> PENDING_COMPLETION or CANCELLED
        -> COMPLETED or DISPUTED
           -> [DISPUTED] -> COMPLETED or CANCELLED
```

1. **CONFIRMED**: The contract is confirmed when the offer is accepted and payment is completed.
2. **IN_PROGRESS**: The contract transitions to in-progress status on the contract start date.
3. **PENDING_COMPLETION**: One party has requested completion.
4. **COMPLETED**: Settlement is processed when both parties agree to completion (terminal state).
5. **DISPUTED**: Transitions to disputed status when there is an objection to completion.
6. **CANCELLED**: The contract has been cancelled (terminal state).
