## Description

PilaMatch is a matching platform that safely connects verified Pilates and Yoga instructors with studios.

Only members who have passed identity verification and business certification can participate. Escrow payments and a no-show penalty system guarantee transaction safety.

### Key Features

- **Verified Members Only**: Only users with confirmed identity (SMS) and business certification can use the service
- **AI-Powered 4-Factor Matching**: Analyzes region, experience, certifications, and desired pay to recommend optimal matches
- **Escrow Payments**: Payment is securely held until contract completion, protecting both parties
- **No-Show Penalty System**: Penalties for unexcused absences ensure a trustworthy transaction environment
- **Trust Score**: Activity-based trust score lets you evaluate counterparts at a glance
- **Premium Membership**: Fee discounts, unlimited applications/views, matching boost, and more

### For Instructors

- Get recommended studios matching your experience and qualifications
- Secure your lesson fees with escrow payments
- Build your Trust Score to receive more offers

### For Studios

- Find the best talent from a pool of verified instructors
- Ensure stable class operations with no-show penalties
- Save hiring time with our matching algorithm
